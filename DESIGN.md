# Montesinos Electric design direction

Use the original wing-shaped mark and real project photos. Preserve “The good guy price” as the memorable hero statement. Use Peralta’s Roofing’s visitor journey: introduction, services, completed work, family story, service area, and contact.

Palette: electric yellow #FFE13B, black #000000, paper #FFFFFF, light grey #F5F5F2, muted copy #575751, divider #DADAD2. Heading font: Barlow Condensed, bold. Body: DM Sans. Left-aligned text, roomy max-width 1280px, a split photo hero, portrait project photography, simple rectangular black buttons with modest corner rounding. No purple.

The photo takes the right half of the opening composition; yellow is reserved for the utility bar, a family-business photo badge, and the final contact section. Distinct service photos and real project captions supply the visual texture. No fabricated ratings, review quotes, response times, warranties, prices, founding date, exact service boundaries, or license numbers.

Contact delivery is intentionally unconfigured per the user. Visitors can prepare and copy project details, then call the verified number. No false success state. Email stays empty in site-content.json. A server endpoint may be configured after ownership/access and provider choices are established.
