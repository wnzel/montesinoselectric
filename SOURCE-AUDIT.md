## Verified content

- Current website describes the business as family-owned, licensed and insured, with nearly 20 years of electrical experience. Preserve that wording; the Google listing separately describes company age and industry experience, so do not imply the company was founded twenty years ago.
- Google listing and user-supplied number: (623) 202-2164. Original footer: (602) 516-4732 and “Espanol: 623-202-2164.” Both numbers are retained, with the Google listing number primary.
- Original footer has a public Gmail address; user explicitly asked to leave email integration empty. Do not enable email delivery.
- Original social icons link to Wix accounts rather than verified Montesinos profiles. Omit them.
- Google listing has no published review score. No ratings, customer quotes, or review counts are invented.
- Exact service coverage and street address were not established by the listing. Use the supplied Phoenix area context; invite visitors to confirm their location by phone. The map shows Phoenix geography, not a claimed radius or coverage boundary.
- Same original logo and 25 existing photographs are retained locally; 20 project photographs are presented in the portfolio. No stock or generated project photography.

## Before the business-domain launch

Confirm the secondary phone, specific service cities, contractor license number if it should be displayed, email ownership/integration, real social profile URLs, and any owner-approved terms. Confirm hours before displaying them; only the listing’s current open state was reviewed. Add no assumed 24/7 service or response-time promises.
